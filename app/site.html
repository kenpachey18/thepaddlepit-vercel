Frequently Asked Questions

Can I cancel my booking?

Bookings are non-cancellable once confirmed.

What happens if it rains during my scheduled session?

If rain affects your booked session, we'll gladly help you reschedule to another available date to use your remaining time.

Can I reschedule my booking?

Yes. If you're unable to make your booking, you may request a reschedule at least two to three days before your scheduled time, subject to court availability.

Can I bring outside food and drinks?

Yes! You're welcome to bring your own food and drinks while enjoying your time at the court. Please help us keep the area clean by disposing of your trash properly.

Is parking available?

Yes, motorcycle parking is available. Please note that the road leading to the court is not accessible to four-wheel vehicles, so we recommend arriving by motorcycle or walking if you're nearby.

How do I know if my booking is confirmed?

After submitting your booking request, our team will review it and confirm your reservation. You can check the status anytime through the My Booking page.

How early should I book?

We recommend booking as early as possible, especially during evenings and weekends, as these time slots tend to fill up quickly.

Can I book multiple hours?

Yes. You can reserve consecutive time slots, subject to availability.

How do I pay for my booking?

Payment is made at the venue after your playing session, unless otherwise advised by the court management.

Can someone else use my booking?

If someone else will be using your reserved slot, please inform us in advance so we can update the booking details if necessary.
